My roadmap to 2030

These are my goals for each year of the degree, not things already done. I will update them as I complete each one.

  1. Year 1 · 2026–27

    Build the foundation

    • Aim for a strong CGPA in the core subjects
    • Learn the basics of pharmacovigilance and drug safety databases by mid 2027
    • Get fluent in MS Excel, Word, PowerPoint and Canva
    • Start GPAT preparation early
  2. Year 2 · 2027–28

    Learn patient care in a hospital

    • Hospital and Clinical Pharmacy internship at Pt. B.D. Sharma PGIMS, Rohtak
    • Practise medication reconciliation, therapeutic drug monitoring and patient counselling
    • Understand regulatory affairs and clinical research (GCP, CDSCO guidelines) by 2028
  3. Year 3 · 2028–29

    Work inside a pharma company

    • Quality Assurance and Formulation internship at Dr. Reddy’s Laboratories
    • Learn pharmaceutical analysis and quality control (HPLC, UV)
    • Add basic data analysis and statistics for pharma
    • Complete a pharmacovigilance and drug safety course
  4. Year 4 · 2029–30

    Step into regulatory work

    • Regulatory Affairs internship at Lupin or Zydus Lifesciences
    • Learn aseptic compounding (IV, IM, TPN, chemotherapy preparation)
    • Sit GPAT and apply for placements, with Pfizer as the top target
  5. After 2030

    Specialise, then build

    • M.Pharm in Pharmacology or Regulatory Affairs, or an MBA in Pharmaceutical Management
    • Clinical Research Associate or Regulatory Affairs Associate role
    • Long term: set up my own pharmaceutical venture
